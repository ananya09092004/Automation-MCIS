# Nexus — customer guide

For people using the Nexus web console in a pilot. Your administrator gives you the console
address and an invitation.

## Get started

1. Sign in with the email address that was invited (email/password or Google).
2. Verify your email if asked; invitations require a verified address.
3. Accept the invitation (link from the email, or the one-time code your admin shares).
4. Pick the workspace in the switcher at the top.

## Daily use

- **Ask Nexus / Create task**: describe the goal in plain language (up to 2000 characters).
  Nexus plans the steps and runs them. Follow progress in **Tasks**.
- **Approvals**: actions rated YELLOW or RED (sending email, payments, deleting data, …) stop
  and wait here. The required role is shown; add a note and approve or reject. Requests expire.
- **Workflows**: run an active workflow with its inputs; see recent runs and their status.
- **Integrations**: connector status and health checks. Connecting accounts is done by an
  admin in the MCIS app.
- **Activity** (admins): audit trail and security events.
- **Usage & Billing**: what your workspace has used against its plan. Payment details appear
  only when your provider has set payments up.
- **Team** (admins): invite members and set roles (owner, admin, member).
- **Settings**: your account, paired desktop devices (revoke a lost laptop here) and, for
  admins, the **emergency stop**, which halts all agent actions in the workspace at once.

## Roles

| Role | Can |
|---|---|
| Member | Create and follow tasks, run workflows, approve YELLOW actions |
| Admin | Everything above, plus agents, team, activity, RED approvals, emergency stop |
| Owner | Everything, including ownership transfer |

## Help

If a page says "not enabled on this server" or "Not configured", that feature has not been
switched on for your organisation yet — contact your administrator.
